A little about us

Food that brings
us together.

At Salt Bread & Spice, we believe good food begins with tradition and craftsmanship. Inspired by the comfort of everyday baking and the soulful character of island seasonings, our kitchen brings people together over food that is simple, generous, and memorable.

We honor Barbadian culinary heritage through scratch-made baking and bold, fire-kissed spices—bringing people together over honest, soulful food that feels like home.

The hands behind the flavor

Meet our chefs

01

Chef Marcus Holder

Head Chef

With over 18 years of experience in Caribbean cuisine and open-fire cooking, Marcus specializes in heritage Bajan comfort dishes and artisanal spice blending. Born and raised in Barbados, he brings deep-rooted culinary tradition to every plate.

02

Chef Alana “Lanie” Clarke

Sous Chef & Baker

Alana is the hands and heart behind our daily bakery operations. With 10 years of experience, she focuses on scratch-made salt breads, slow fermentation, and traditional island baking.

03

Chef Josiah “Jo” Browne

Spice & Seafood Chef

Josiah brings vibrant creativity to the kitchen, specializing in house-made pepper sauces, marinades, fresh coastal seafood pairings, and the art of island spice.
